# T16c: bounded TN 1270 closure pass

## 1. Verdict, scope and reading rules

**NO-GO for a fully sourced experimental comparison.** Reference definitions and published-report reuse are closed; continuous spanwise prescriptions and matched section inputs are only partly closed; exact model-specific Mach remains unverified. Useful section plots were found, but they do not establish the selected wing's complete input set. This verdict concerns source sufficiency, not the correctness of the experiment or an accuracy threshold. (§§3-5 below; T pp. 3-6, Tables I-II, Figs. 1/3/4; N p. 20, Table IV, Figs. 1-3.)

The owner has selected **wing-alone** as the first experimental comparison, with TN 1270 prepared **conditionally**. RM SL56H06 is excluded from that comparison and retained as a v0.2 source candidate because its tests are supersonic; CRM-HL is excluded because of deployed slats. No modeling starts before the checkpoint in [ESTIMATOR_PLAN.md](ESTIMATOR_PLAN.md#current-scope-and-pre-modeling-checkpoint--t16c). These are recorded owner directions, not new decisions by this audit. (Owner's T16c request, “Decisions so far”; [T16b](T16B_REFERENCE_SEARCH.md) §§5-6/9; F pp. 2-5; O §6.4 p. 3.)

This was one closure pass with a twenty-minute source-search cap: reread TN 1270, follow its design/calculation references TN 713, Report 703 and TN 1269, and inspect the related TN 1677 follow-up. The pass ends here rather than expanding into another candidate search. No tables were numerically transcribed, no model inputs selected, and no solver or experimental comparison run. “Open” means not established in the inspected locations, not that the information cannot exist elsewhere. Printed pages are used below; figure-only sheets also give PDF positions. (This audit's procedure; T reference list p. 12; F7 Introduction p. 2; T16b §1.)

The report gives airfoil-family and root/tip identities; the gap is their continuous realization and matched section laws, not an entirely unnamed airfoil. (T p. 4/Table I; N p. 20/Table IV; this audit §3.)

## 6. Consequence for v0.1 and the Stage 3 route

Under the owner's stated consequence for a NO-GO or assumption-dependent result, the v0.1 estimator must be labeled **“code-to-code cross-checked, not experimentally validated.”** This is the required reporting category, not a claim that an AVL/OpenVSP cross-check was run here. The evidence currently available is the repository's analytic/synthetic checks and archived independent-solver comparisons; any public claim must name those actual comparisons and their restrictions. A future AVL/OpenVSP comparison needs matched geometry, section assumptions, reference axes/area/chord, wake settings, numerical convergence and artifact permission review. Code agreement alone does not close the experimental gaps. (Owner T16c requirement 4; T16b §8; V §Introduction; PLAN Later AVL cross-check and artifact policy; [T15b production comparison](../data/reference/estimator/t15b_production_comparison.md), Which implementation ran/Measured comparison/Verification and limits.)

**Moving on to Stage 3 requires the owner's checkpoint response, then a separately authorized task defining the designer/report interface and its evidence labels.** The roadmap's parametric-parts and headless-report work can be planned around existing capabilities and explicit unavailable outputs. It must not silently generate missing whole-aircraft derivatives, stall predictions or an experimental-validation claim from this wing-only audit. Keep sourced inputs, proposed assumptions and missing capabilities visible in the report; T17 and the deferred wing-tail/body/finite-Mach work do not start merely because Stage 3 is the recommended next direction. T10/T11 and D8/D9 are unchanged. (Owner T16c Decisions/requirement 4; ROADMAP Stage 3; PLAN Current scope and §§T17-T24; T16b §§7-8.)
