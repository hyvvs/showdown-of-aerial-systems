# F-104 / Navion: go or no go

**Navion first; neither is ready for aircraft validation.** Navion has subsonic longitudinal/lateral data; F-104 lacks CR lateral data and most conditions exceed the simulator's envelope (CR pp. 74, 76-78, 100-102; FMT §“Table layout and interpolation contract”). This audits the requested derivative comparison; queued T10 remains power-off stall/glide/trim/static margin (TASKS §T10).

**T10:** **Navion** permits wing-only CL_alpha (VLM/LLT), Cm_alpha (VLM); aircraft comparison needs new tail/downwash/fuselage work. **F-104** permits the same wing quantities with additional geometry/low-AR limitations; transonic/supersonic comparison needs appropriate new compressible/supersonic physics. All control/beta/rate derivatives above need estimator work (VLM/LLT; CR pp. 74, 77, 101; [Caughey](https://courses.cit.cornell.edu/mae5070/Caughey_2011_04.pdf) §§2.5-2.7, pp. 19-21). Neither wing result validates whole-aircraft slopes; induced drag supplies neither total CD_alpha nor stall. Published CL_alpha-dot/CL_M/CL_delta_e, CD_alpha/CD_M/CD_delta_e, Cm_alpha-dot/Cm_M and extra lateral-control terms also exceed current simulator equations (CR pp. 77, 101; VLM; FMT §“Dynamic derivatives and limits: version 2 (T9b)”).

**T11:** **Navion:** simulator can integrate supplied subsonic coefficients; missing table inputs and a single exact-node envelope block a sourced flight across speeds/heights. **F-104:** only CR takeoff is below the Mach ceiling; missing lateral/tensor/control inputs, variant matching and broader flight physics block completion (CR pp. 74, 76-79, 100-103; FMT §§“Fields, units and reference frames”, “Table layout and interpolation contract”).

**Ranked blockers / unknowns:** (1) missing complete inputs/power-off observations and F/H/S variant matching (CR pp. 74-79, 100-103; H pp. 2, 5-6; S pp. 1-4); (2) axes/control/reference conversion, S's axial-force sign ambiguity, inertia-product signs and missing physics (CR A-1/A-4/B-1; S pp. 2-3; VLM/LLT); (3) Navion area conflict/blank X-C trim alpha, F-104 condition-1 Mach conflict and area-precision provenance (CR pp. 74, 76-79, 100-101); (4) S's reversed Reynolds/Mach pairing, blocking unqualified tunnel attribution (S pp. 2, 5). Scan-visible discrepancies remain unresolved, not OCR-corrected. Navion first reduces scope; neither variant identity nor missing power-off evidence was determined. H3/H4, D8/D9 and matching JSBSim runs remain open (same pages; TASKS §H3/H4/T10/T11).

**Flight versus tunnel:** L's swept-wing A/B are not identified F-104/Navion data: approximately A Mach 0.7-1.44, B 0.7-1.23, near 35,000-ft pressure altitude; measured tunnel data exclude Mach 1.03-1.12 (pp. 6-8, 12, 15-16). A's CL_alpha reasonably agrees; tunnel static margin is about five percent of chord lower, transonic break Mach 0.90 versus flight 0.85. B's flight CL_alpha is about five percent lower; tunnel static margin is described as five-to-ten percent smaller (percentage basis not restated), not a universal Cm_alpha percentage error (p. 13; Figs. 8-9 pp. 28-29). Cn_beta/Cl_beta levels generally agree but B's Mach trends differ; flight CY_beta magnitude is usually about twenty percent smaller (pp. 14-15; Figs. 10-11 pp. 30-31). The fifty-percent-or-larger damping-error warning concerns identification uncertainty in Cl_p/combined yaw damping, not measured tunnel disagreement; B's pitch damping over Mach 0.95-1.23 is contaminated by control motion (pp. 12, 16). No common acceptance tolerance follows (L pp. 12-16; TASKS §H3/T10).
