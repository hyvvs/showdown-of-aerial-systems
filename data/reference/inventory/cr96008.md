# CR-96008: F-104 and Navion source inventory

## Scope and retrieval record

Documentation inventory only. No coefficient, geometry, weight, inertia or
transfer-function values have been transcribed into a data set. Numbers below
identify pages, conditions and coverage; they are not calibrated model inputs.
H3/H4 acceptance, aircraft selection and validation tolerances remain for the
owner (`docs/TASKS.md`, H3/H4; `docs/DECISIONS.md`, D8/D9).

Primary source: Gary L. Teper, **Aircraft Stability and Control Data**,
NASA CR-96008 / STI TR-176-1, **April 1969**. Title, author and date were
checked on the scanned cover, PDF page 1. The title/date match
[NTRS 19690022405](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19690022405).
[Retrieved PDF](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19690022405/downloads/19690022405.pdf?attachment=true).

Read on 2026-11-19: printed pp. 1-2, 73-79, 99-103, Appendix A pp. A-1-A-10
and Appendix B p. B-1. The relevant table, drawing and sign-definition pages
were also inspected as rendered images. **Could not open:** none of these
primary-source pages. The bare web URL initially returned an access error;
the downloadable PDF was retrieved successfully. Other requested reports and
their access records are in [other_sources.md](other_sources.md).

Page citations below use the printed report number. PDF page numbers are
one-based viewer positions, including covers and blank pages. For the two
aircraft sections, PDF page = printed page + 6; Appendix A starts at PDF 121
and Appendix B at PDF 131. These locations were checked against the scanned
page footers (pp. 73-79, 99-103, A-1, B-1).

The report is a handling-qualities compilation, not a flight-test report for
each aircraft. Its introduction explicitly allows incomplete aircraft coverage,
retains the source's axes for nondimensional derivatives, and warns that some
conditions/configurations are early estimates. Do not label the following
tables measured flight data merely because they are in this compilation
(pp. 1-2, including the footnote on p. 1).

### What supports a power-off comparison?

**No listed F-104 condition is a documented power-off validation case.** The
condition/configuration tables contain no engine setting, zero-thrust statement,
idle/windmilling drag correction or measured engine-off performance. The
condition-1 flight path is a climb; the remaining tabulated flight paths are
level. None is labeled a glide or stall run (pp. 74, 76-79).

The following are review inferences about what a later estimator would need
to predict, not claims that the report supplies those missing measurements.

| Requested comparison | Usable F-104 condition(s) and evidence | Predictions needed, and why this is not yet a power-off validation |
|---|---|---|
| Stall | None established. Condition 1 is a takeoff operating point, not a maximum-lift or stall-speed measurement (pp. 74, 76-77). | Predict CL versus alpha through its maximum and the corresponding stall speed at matched weight, density and flap state. The section supplies local CL/slope and a loading point, but no CLmax, stall criterion or engine-off stall observation (pp. 74, 76-79). |
| Glide | None established. Conditions 2-5 give operating-point CL/CD and nominally level flight, not a descending engine-off trajectory (VII-A/B, pp. 76-77). | Predict total aircraft CL and CD versus alpha/speed, trimmed moment and control position, then glide angle and sink rate under a documented propulsion-off drag condition. ## Axes, signs and later transformation evidence

| Evidence | Source definition and consequence |
|---|---|
| Appendix A, A-1 / PDF 121 | Body axes are right-handed, fixed to the aircraft, centered at nominal CG, with X along the body centerline reference. Stability axes are a **particular body-fixed frame** whose X aligns with the projection of the **steady-state** total velocity on the symmetry plane. This is not a frame that continuously follows perturbed velocity. |
| Appendix A, A-2-A-4 / PDF 122-124 | X/velocity positive forward, Y positive out the right wing, Z positive down; positive roll is right wing down, pitch nose up, yaw nose right. Lift is positive up and drag positive aft. The symbol N is used both for upward normal force and yaw moment; resolve it by context. |
| VII-A-D pp. 76-79; X-A-C p. 101 | F-104 tables explicitly say body-fixed stability axes. Navion X-A says body-fixed stability axes; X-B/C say stability axes. Do not relabel these as body-centerline derivatives without the nominal rotation. |
| Appendix A, A-6-A-7; Appendix B, B-1 / PDF 126-127, 131 | The source body-axis normal coefficient is positive up and axial coefficient positive aft, unlike its signed force components Z and X. Stability-to-body derivative transformations, including lateral roll/yaw mixing, are provided in B-1. Retain alpha and coefficient normalization with every later transcription. |
| Appendix A, A-2, A-10 | Inertias/products and inertia-coupled primed derivatives are defined. The symbol list calls Ixz a product of inertia; it does not explicitly state an integral-sign convention. A later signed tensor conversion must verify that convention from the defining equations/source rather than copy a sign into D11 by name alone. |

Exact control sign evidence, common to the compilation (A-4 / PDF 124):

| Control | Positive sign actually defined in CR-96008 | Physical-surface evidence still missing here |
|---|---|---|
| Aileron, delta-a | Positive for a positive rolling moment; definition includes spoiler effects. Combined with A-2/A-3, that moment is right wing down. | A-4 does not specify left/right trailing-edge travel or the half-difference versus total-difference convention. |
| Elevator, delta-e | Deflection **from trim**, positive for nose-down pitching moment for an aft surface. Trim deflection is a separate symbol. | A-4 does not state a trailing-edge direction or identify the F-104 all-moving-tail mechanism. |
| Rudder, delta-r | Positive for nose-left yawing moment, explicitly negative yaw moment. | A-4 does not state trailing-edge travel direction. |

These moment-sign definitions are evidence relevant to D6, not independent
proof of all of D6's physical trailing-edge directions. Leading/trailing-edge
**flap** settings in Fig. VII-1 are separate quantities; their signs must not
be substituted for elevator, aileron or rudder signs (A-4; p. 74).
